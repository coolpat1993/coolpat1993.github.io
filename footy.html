How To Play

Quick Guide

Play cards from your hand each turn using Energy (3 per turn). Attack cards score goals by reducing the opponent's Pressure (HP); reach the required goals to win a match. Use Defence and Tactical cards to manage Block and setup combos. Between matches you can pick rewards, visit shops and manage your deck.
Buffs & Debuffs — Quick Keywords
Momentum (Buff): Increases your attack effectiveness; stacks add small bonuses.
Vulnerable / Defensive Gap (Debuff on enemy): Enemy takes increased damage.
Weak / Under Pressure (Debuff on player): Reduces damage dealt by attacks.
Frail / Disorganised: Reduces the effectiveness of Block (defence).
Fatigue: Deals damage over time at end of turns.
Shaken: Temporarily reduces the opponent's next attack (or their next action effectiveness).
