Cast quiz image maker

Paste an IMDb link, choose how many cast members to show, and download a 1280 × 1840 image. The film's title is never drawn on it.

Optional: paste IMDb page source instead

Open the IMDb page, press Ctrl+U (Cmd+Option+U on Mac), copy everything and paste it here. This reads IMDb's own cast list and order.

Tick or untick people to fine-tune. Use “Replace” to swap in your own photo.
